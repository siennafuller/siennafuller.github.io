Art

'Window 2.0, 1&2' (2023)

Glass casting

35x40cm

 

By prioritizing fluidity, "Window 2.0" invites viewers to consider the interplay between structure and form in a new light. The architectural elements, rather than being confined to rigid boundaries, flow and interact, reflecting the complexities of contemporary architecture and design.

This approach not only challenges conventional notions of framing but also encourages a dialogue about the spaces we inhabit and how they influence our perceptions.

The result is a piece that embodies both strength and grace, inviting contemplation on the relationship between form and function while celebrating the beauty of architectural innovation.

Ultimately, "Window 2.0" transforms the concept of a window from a static element into a dynamic experience, engaging viewers in a conversation about the nature of space and perception.
